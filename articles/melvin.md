## Introduction

This article intends to fill out some of the blanks when it comes to what 'Melvin' is, and what we do. It'll likely be appended over-time with new information if we deem it fit.
I, the author view Melvin more as a 'we' than an 'i' or individual project, primarily due to the fact that the community has had just as much of a hand in it as i have, and labelling it as any less would be discrediting.

Melvin initially began as a counter to the various minimal effort 'vibecoded' 'slop' bots that plauge discord in 2026, that can't really apply to the everyday users needs, and that tend to be littered with inconsistencies, and bugs, and that is still the case. We remain very transparent on our AI use cases within documentation, with [documentation sections](/docs/claude) dedicated to labelling our AI use cases.
It was also always our goal to try and ring in as many rouge contributors as possible, all covering different area's of the project, to keep it maintained, and fresh, and as of 16/11/2026 there are a total of 5 active contributors, which is mind blowing.

Melvin utilises Python 3.14 & Discord.py framework, primarily because it's widely adapted within the Discord bot space, and despite it's flaws, it's quite easy to develop with > drastically improving development time, and debugging times. It also allows us to bring in the most contributors, because as mentioned earlier, it's so widely adapted > However later down the line, i do see there being potential ports of Melvin in other languages.

## Timeline
### August
Melvin initially began early-mid august, though i had purchased the domain 'justmelvin.site' a couple months prior to this. It was always intended to be a modular Discord bot, and i had already mapped that out at this point, though it wouldn't come to fruition until later on.
Late august, the first few commits had finally begun, and FinestPerfectionism had actually taught me how to use github at this point (i had no prior experience). He would (and still does) format and lint any commits i make, and would also double check them to ensure efficiency, and quality. FinestPerfectionism was a key part of what actually kept Melvin updated, stable, and growing. I feel as if i would have lost motivation much sooner if it had not been for him.
### September
September is where things started to set in stone, for example, this is when the first iteration of our fox branding was set in place, to replace the old 'M' logo. This was much more marketable, and despite some community pushback, it eventually stuck and hasn't really changed much since. This is also where we started to branch out into different cogs, with the tool cog, ai cog, and mod cog finally starting to take shape here. However towards the end of the month, activity slightly dropped in terms of commits, as i had become pre-occupied with university engagements, with it having around 90 commits over last months 130+.
This was also the month where DinoCDX made his first public appearence, though he would not fully join the team until next month.
### October
October was the first iteration of what has now become our website. Note that at this time there was no dashboard, it was just a landing page, meant soley for advertising purposes, as well as documentation for each of our cogs / modules which we would soon refer to them as.
It wasn't until late october that the dashboard had finally started to take shape, thanks to me re-figuring out discord Oauth. We then used the dashboard to allow users to configure their modules, by linking states between the bot, and the site process via SQLite, and setting up identical configuration options on both the site, and discord, to be as user facing as possible. It's also around this time we finally saw DinoCDX join the team, as he made a suprise appearance. Due to the dashboard implementation, comment activity skyrocketed this month.
### November
November was a slower month, atleast towards the start, but that was mainly at fault of the new UI, and article handling being worked on in the background. It's always been a goal of mine to create a solid, and reputable brand image, and that's what i put time into accomplishing with this stream of commits. As i'm writing this, it's mid november, and we already have two new contributors, one from early november, one from literally yesterday, Fantastic-Fanta, and ImKajii. Having such a large team of contributors helps new ideas consistently flow in, and activity remain at a stable rate, which is great for keeping everyone motivated to work on this project.

## What's next?
It's difficult to say what's next for Melvin, we usually act quite quickly on ideas, usually within the same day / week if possible. As stated above, this article will be updated as time goes on, to reflect Melvin in it's current state. If there's anything missing from this article please let me know, and feel free to point out any mistakes. If you're reading this and you'd like to become a contributor, all it takes is one PR, if it gets merged, you're with us! Thanks for your time, and we hope we can continue providing Melvin to you all.
