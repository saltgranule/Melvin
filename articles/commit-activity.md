Hey peeps, we'll be using this new article format for minor announcements and discussions for Melvin. Just posting this article to discuss commit activity within this project. I hope you enjoy the various new animated graphics and gifs, it's been a long standing effort to improve Melvins brand image, to reflect something reputable, and consistent.

## number stuff

## why some months are quieter

Even though it may look like activity drops in certain months, this is primarily due to education, with me being in university, and other contributors also being busy with education. However as you can see by the thumbnail visual, we do always make a return.
A quieter month in commits doesn't mean nothing is happening either. Some of the biggest changes land as a single commit after a lot of work offline, like this month's site and dashboard refurbish

## what went into each month

**August** was the beginning of melvin, small commits, ranging from decoding commands, and the foundation of the tool module, to the ai module, and so much more. At this point, the website didn't even exist yet, but melvin had already caught the eye of some our contributors.

**September** gave melvin the status page, giving it latency and uptime statistics, and components that would later be used to create server stats panel

**October** brought audit logs posted through webhooks thanks to DinoCDX, custom commands with their own dashboard page, reminders, better error handling across every command, and followup and retry buttons for /ai ask thanks to Lapras, with sources listed for searched answers.

**November so far** has added the AFK module, grouped settings on the dashboard, and a full redesign of the website and dashboard, along with a donations page and these articles.

That brings Melvin to 13 modules, from moderation and audit logs to timezones, starboards and thanks. I also cannot stress this is enough, a good chunk of these were only possible thanks to [our contributors](https://github.com/saltgranule/Melvin/graphs/contributors), as i wouldn't have thought of it, or would have been too busy.

## what's next

Melvin will continue to be maintained, and updated with the latest Discord API features. Smaller announcements and discussions like this one will be posted here as articles. PS this is quite a small article, there's not much to cover on this topic, i just felt as if something had to be said, as Melvin does go through activity droughts quite frequently.
